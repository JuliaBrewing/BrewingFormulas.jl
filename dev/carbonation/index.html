Carbonation

The CO₂ left in the beer after fermentation, the priming sugar to reach a given carbonation, and the pressure for forced carbonation.

BrewingFormulas.residual_co2 — Function
residual_co2(temperature)

The CO₂, in vols, remaining dissolved in a beer after fermentation at temperature (the highest temperature reached after fermentation), at 1 atm: 3.0378 - 0.050062 T + 0.00026555 T², with T in °F.

References

  • M. L. Hall, "Brew By the Numbers — Add Up What's in Your Beer", Zymurgy, Summer 1995, pp. 54–61, Equation 26.
julia> residual_co2(20u"°C")
0.8614871999999993 vols
source
BrewingFormulas.priming_sugar — Function
priming_sugar(sugar, volume, target, temperature)

The mass, in g, of priming sugar to bring a volume of beer, fermented up to temperature, to the target carbonation (in vols), using 1 vols = 1.977 g/L of CO₂.

The mass of sugar follows from the stoichiometry of its fermentation (see co2_yield). Hall's formula PS = 15.195 V(gal) (CD - CD₀), in grams of corn sugar, corresponds to anhydrous glucose; here CornSugar is dextrose monohydrate, as usually sold, which needs about 10% more. Use Glucose for Hall's values.

References

  • M. L. Hall, "Brew By the Numbers — Add Up What's in Your Beer", Zymurgy, Summer 1995, pp. 54–61, Equation 29.
julia> priming_sugar(CornSugar(), 20u"L", 2.5u"vols", 20u"°C")
145.86469026359262 g

BrewingFormulas.co2_yield — Function
co2_yield(sugar)

Mass of CO₂ produced per mass of the priming sugar, assuming full fermentation, from the stoichiometry of the fermentation and the molar masses of the sugar and of CO₂ (IUPAC standard atomic weights).

source
BrewingFormulas.carbonation_pressure — Function
carbonation_pressure(level, temperature)

The gauge pressure, in psi, of CO₂ to force carbonate a beer at temperature to the given carbonation level (in vols), from the fit of the carbonation charts used by most brewing calculators (T in °F, V in vols, P in psig):

P = -16.6999 - 0.0101059 T + 0.00116512 T² + 0.173354 T V + 4.24267 V - 0.0684226 V².
julia> carbonation_pressure(2.5u"vols", 38u"°F")
11.246172830000003 psi

References

  • No primary source for this fit was found; it is the "standard force carbonation equation" of many brewing calculators, a fit to the published carbonation charts (CO₂ solubility tables such as those of the ASBC).
source
BrewingFormulas.carbonation_level — Function
carbonation_level(pressure, temperature)

The carbonation, in vols, reached at equilibrium under the gauge pressure of CO₂ at temperature; the inverse of carbonation_pressure.
