Notes on the Python BrewQuiver

  • Its abv_via_extrato_final_aparente passed the apparent final extract to conteudo_alcoolico_peso, which expects the real one; abv here uses the real extract, as in Balling's relation.
  • Its options "Druey-Daniels" and "Druey-Noonan" are Kyle Druey's power fits, and are DrueyDaniels and DrueyNoonan here.
  • Its Tinseth utilization was divided by 0.9 ("since Tinseth is for leaf"), with a factor 0.9 for leaf hops; here Tinseth's model is kept as published, and hop forms are handled by the factor keyword.
