Yeast

The number of yeast cells to pitch.

BrewingFormulas.pitch_cells — Function
pitch_cells(volume, gravity; rate = 0.75, eq = SugarGravityQuad())

The number of yeast cells to pitch in a volume of wort of the given gravity, at rate million cells per mL per °P (usually 0.75 for ales and 1.5 for lagers).

References

  • The rates 0.75 (ales) and 1.5 (lagers) million cells/mL/°P are commonly attributed to G. Fix, as in C. White and J. Zainasheff, Yeast: The Practical Guide to Beer Fermentation, Brewers Publications, 2010.
julia> pitch_cells(20u"L", 12u"°P")
1.8e11
