Color

Malt color units (MCU), the color of the grains times their mass over the volume of wort, are additive; they are converted to the SRM color of the wort by one of the color models.

BrewingFormulas.mcu — Function
mcu(mass, color, volume)
mcu(bill, volume)

The malt color units, MCU = mass(lb) × color(°L) / volume(gal), of a mass of grain of the given color (in °L, SRM or EBC) in a volume of wort. With a bill, an iterable of mass => color pairs, the sum of the MCUs of the grains. MCUs are additive, unlike SRM.

References

  • D. Morey, "Approximating SRM Beer Color of Homebrew Based on Recipe Formulation", 2015, https://bwelman.github.io/bier/beer-color/beer-color.html.
julia> mcu(4u"kg", 3u"°L", 20u"L")
5.007242671211599
source
BrewingFormulas.srm — Function
srm([model = Morey()], mcu)

The color, in SRM, of a wort with the given malt color units mcu, with the given ColorModel.

julia> srm(10)
7.239807780133753 SRM
source
BrewingFormulas.wort_color — Function
wort_color([model = Morey()], bill, volume)

The color, in SRM, of a volume of wort with the grains of bill, an iterable of mass => color pairs: srm(model, mcu(bill, volume)).

source
BrewingFormulas.srm_rgb — Function
srm_rgb(color)

An (r, g, b) tuple of integers in 0:255 representing a beer of the given color (in SRM, EBC or °L), interpolated in a table from 1 to 40 SRM; colors outside this range are clamped to it.

References

  • The table comes from the Python BrewQuiver, which gives no source for it, and no source was found. For a physically based alternative, see A. J. deLange's spectral model of beer color, implemented e.g. in https://pypi.org/project/olfarve/.
source
BrewingFormulas.srm_hex — Function
srm_hex(color)

The hexadecimal RGB string, as "#rrggbb", of srm_rgb(color).
