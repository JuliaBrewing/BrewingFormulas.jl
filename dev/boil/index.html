Boil and volumes

The density of water as a function of temperature, used for the thermal contraction of wort, and the evaporation in the boil.

BrewingFormulas.water_density — Function
water_density(temperature)

The density of water, in kg/L, at the given temperature, by Kell's formula (1975), valid from 0 to 150 °C.

References

  • G. S. Kell, "Density, thermal expansivity, and compressibility of liquid water from 0° to 150 °C", J. Chem. Eng. Data 20 (1975) 97–105, https://doi.org/10.1021/je60064a005.
julia> water_density(20u"°C")
0.9982041322005837 kg L⁻¹
source
BrewingFormulas.volume_at_temperature — Function
volume_at_temperature(volume, from, to)

The volume, at temperature to, of a volume of liquid measured at temperature from, taking its thermal expansion as that of water. E.g. the shrinkage of a wort when cooled.

julia> volume_at_temperature(25u"L", 100u"°C", 20u"°C")
24.002196197557904 L
source
BrewingFormulas.evaporated_volume — Function
evaporated_volume(volume, rate, time)

The volume evaporated from a volume of boiling wort during time, with the evaporation rate either as a volume per time (e.g. 4u"L/hr") or as a fraction of volume per time (e.g. 10u"percent/hr").
