Bitterness

The utilization of alpha acids in the boil, given by one of the bitterness models, and the resulting bitterness, taking 1 IBU as 1 mg/L of iso-alpha acids. The isomerization below boiling (hop stands, whirlpools) is given by the rate of isomerization relative to boiling.

BrewingFormulas.utilization — Function
utilization([model = Tinseth()], gravity, time; eq = SugarGravityQuad())

The fraction of alpha acids isomerized in a wort of the given (boil) gravity after boiling for time, with the given BitternessModel; see the models for the references.

julia> utilization(1.050u"sg", 60u"minute")
0.2306640772510991
source
BrewingFormulas.isomerization_rate — Function
isomerization_rate(temperature)

The first-order rate constant, per minute, of the isomerization of alpha acids into iso-alpha acids in wort at temperature, by the Arrhenius fit of Malowicki and Shellhammer: k = 7.9 × 10¹¹ exp(-11858 K / T) per minute, about 0.0125 per minute at 100 °C. See isomerization_factor.

References

  • M. G. Malowicki and T. H. Shellhammer, "Isomerization and Degradation Kinetics of Hop (Humulus lupulus) Acids in a Model Wort-Boiling System", J. Agric. Food Chem. 53 (2005) 4434–4439, https://doi.org/10.1021/jf0481296. The constants are those quoted from this paper in brewing calculators; they were not checked against the paper itself.
julia> ustrip(u"minute^-1", isomerization_rate(100u"°C"))   # per minute, at 100 °C
0.01249025501937107
source
BrewingFormulas.isomerization_factor — Function
isomerization_factor(temperature; reference = 100u"°C")

The rate of isomerization of alpha acids at temperature relative to the rate at reference (boiling), by the Arrhenius fit of isomerization_rate: about 0.71 at 96 °C, 0.42 at 90 °C and 0.20 at 82 °C, and negligible below 60 °C. A hop stand or whirlpool of time t at temperature then isomerizes about as much as a boil of isomerization_factor(temperature) * t.

julia> isomerization_factor(90u"°C")
0.41683354891657887
source

BrewingFormulas.isomerized_alpha_acids — Function
isomerized_alpha_acids([model = Tinseth()], mass, alpha, gravity, time; factor = 1, eq = SugarGravityQuad())

The mass, in mg, of iso-alpha acids from a mass of hops with alpha acid content alpha, boiled for time in a wort of the given gravity, with the utilization given by the BitternessModel times factor.

source
BrewingFormulas.ibu — Function
ibu(isoalpha, volume)
ibu([model = Tinseth()], mass, alpha, gravity, time, volume; factor = 1, eq = SugarGravityQuad())

The bitterness, in IBU, of a volume of wort with the mass isoalpha of iso-alpha acids, 1 IBU being taken as 1 mg/L, the usual approximation. In the second form, the iso-alpha acids come from a mass of hops with alpha acid content alpha boiled for time in a wort of the given gravity, as in isomerized_alpha_acids.

julia> ibu(30u"g", 10u"percent", 1.050u"sg", 60u"minute", 20u"L")
34.59961158766487 IBU
